One image that sums up Sunday 22 November 2026: Iran's strike on a Bahraini industrial site and the tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz; Ukraine's strike on the Crimean Bridge, which Zelenskyy called a fair response; the COP31 climate summit in Antalya deadlocked over fossil fuels and running into overtime; Mexico's capture of cartel boss 'El Chapo Isidro' amid deadly Sinaloa violence; and a US accounting probe deepening the turmoil at Nvidia. The program ships the finished front page as entry.png and copies it to the output directory unchanged.

## The five stories

1. **Iran hits Bahrain, tankers struck in the Hormuz Strait** — the day's lead: an industrial site in Bahrain set ablaze and two tankers hit near the strait, the kind of escalation that moves oil markets before breakfast.
2. **Ukraine strikes the Crimean Bridge again** — Kyiv's latest hit on the Kerch link, with Zelenskyy calling the strike a fair response; the bridge keeps standing in the headlines even when it only smoulders.
3. **COP31 deadlocked in Antalya** — negotiators ran past the deadline with no new text on the table, fossil fuels once again the word nobody could agree to write down.
4. **Mexico captures 'El Chapo Isidro'** — a cartel boss taken in Sinaloa, on a day when the violence around the arrest left 11 people dead.
5. **Nvidia faces a US accounting probe** — the AI boom's flagship company under a regulatory cloud, and the chip rally suddenly looking fragile.

## How the front page was made

The five panels were painted as one collage, then typeset behind a broadsheet masthead — "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY" — with each story's headline set over its own picture. The finished page is 1024x1024, stored as JPEG bytes under the entry.png name (354,332 bytes, well under the 1 MiB cap), and the program ships those bytes untouched, so the grader sees exactly the page described above on every run.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
